# THE PLAYBOOK (gold / XAUUSD, intraday) — all times Dubai

Grades: A+ and A only get called. B is logged, never sent. If unsure, it is not A.

## Strategies (tiers from the 1-year backtest + live results)
- S/A+ — Prior-day breakout + RETEST: price breaks the prior-day high/low, comes back to the line, holds (1-min close outside). No retest = no trade. Never chase the break.
- S/A+ — Continuation breakout: M15 candle closes past the 20-candle high/low with the M15 AND 4H trend agreeing. Stop beyond the breakout candle.
- A — Continuation long / short (breakout close only, trend alive — confirm the trend is NOT over before any continuation).
- A — Gold pocket (0.705–0.79) / Silver pocket (0.618–0.705) of the 4H fib swing: wait for price to hit the zone, reject it and turn — confirmed by a 1–2 minute candle closing back the other way (green for longs, red for shorts). Never blind-fill at the level. Skip if price grinds into the zone and sits there. Stop starts at the FAR edge of the zone (beyond any push past it).
- A — 30-min box retest: box = high/low of the 03:00–03:30 Dubai candle (02:00–02:30 when the US is on summer time). First clean break per side only, breakout candle volume ≥1.2× average, 4H trend not against it. Enter on the box line, stop at the middle of the box, target 3R. If price closes back inside, that side is done for the session.
- A — Liquidity sweep of the day high/low or prior-day high/low: price pokes past where the stops sit, a 1–2 min candle closes back inside. Stop beyond the sweep wick.
- B — First-hour box, fade the failed breakout (log only).
- In a clear range: a planned long (or short) between the range edges is allowed, with a planned entry and exit.
- CUT (never call): buying 20-EMA pullbacks, hammer/pin bar/doji as a long trigger, catching bottoms against the trend, wick trades, chop.

## Candle checks (on top of every strategy)
- Act on candle CLOSES, never on a wick through a level.
- Skip small breakout candles (< 1.2× normal size) unless volume > 1.8× average.
- The next candle decides: closes past the breakout candle = valid; closes back inside = no trade.
- Read all timeframes together (1m, 5m, 15m, 1h, 4h, daily) — never one candle alone.

## Stops and targets
- Stops go BEYOND where the crowd's stops are ("past the gate"), never on an obvious level. 1–2 points is normal gold noise.
- Stops are structure-based: beyond the prior swing high (shorts) or swing low (longs), never a uniform $ or point amount.
- First target must be more than 5 points away. Give TP1 and TP2.
- On big size, bank profit early at TP1.

## Never
- No new trade within 20 minutes of a high-impact USD release (CPI, NFP, FOMC, etc.), and none until the first 1-min candle after it has closed.
- No counter-trend continuation; no trades in chop.
- If the setups don't come, we don't trade. A flat day is fine.
